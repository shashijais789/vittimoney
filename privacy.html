Privacy Policy

The short version

VittiMoney has no accounts, collects no personal data and sends nothing you type to any server. Your calculator inputs stay on your device.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit personal data, financial information or calculator inputs. There are no sign-ups, no forms and no user database.

Local storage

To remember your last calculator inputs across visits on the same device, VittiMoney uses your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your browser, is not readable by this site's server, and you can clear it anytime through your browser's settings.

Third parties

This site is served by a static file host (GitHub Pages) which, like any web host, records routine technical logs (e.g. IP addresses and requested pages) for security and reliability. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or third-party tracking scripts.
